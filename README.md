# Growth Audit — Kindroid

**Live:** https://hribeiro-adapty.github.io/kendroid-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Kindroid already has, plus a clickable prototype of the iOS onboarding and trial sheet rebuilt in Kindroid's own design. Only the order of the screens changed. On a phone, open the link with `?open=proto` to land straight on the prototype.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Where it lands | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Give a second chance when they close the trial: a plain free-vs-Premium table and Monthly at R$ 99,90 with no trial | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Let people meet a Kindroid before the login, and ask for the account after the paywall | Welcome, Account | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 03 | Put the Kindroid they just made on the paywall, after a short setup screen that reads their choices back | Setup, Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Stop showing a quarterly plan that costs more per month than Monthly on the Brazilian App Store | Paywall | ARPU +10–15% |
| 05 | Show the 4.8 from 11K ratings and the press coverage up front | Welcome | CR +10–15% |

## Grounding

Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from Kindroid's own published material: the in-app screens (Brazilian App Store storefront), the App Store listing, kindroid.ai and the Kindroid help centre (Subscriptions page). Nothing is invented: no discounts, no countdowns, no statistics.

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Kindroid.
