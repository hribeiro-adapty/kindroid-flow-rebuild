What this is

We opened Kindroid the way a new user does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Kindroid: installed it, went from the login to the trial sheet, read your App Store listing and your help centre, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you currently show people.

What follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours: the warm cream background, the four-step progress bar, the black pill buttons, the community cards with their tag chips, the dark trial banner and your Today, Day 2, Day 3 timeline. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
